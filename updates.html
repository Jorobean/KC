KEMS opens its early-access waitlist

November 9, 2026

We’ve updated getkems.com with a clearer introduction to KEMS and opened an email waitlist for early-access news.

What is KEMS?

KEMS (Kinetic Energy Management System) helps you capture everyday experiences, revisit meaningful moments, and turn them into something worth keeping or sharing. We’re building a more personal way to record, reflect, and create.

That focus brings together the moments you want to remember and the ideas you want to develop. An everyday experience can be worth returning to, whether you want to keep it for yourself or create something to share.

What does joining the waitlist mean?

The waitlist is for KEMS early-access news. Add your email address through the form on our homepage to receive updates. You can unsubscribe anytime.
